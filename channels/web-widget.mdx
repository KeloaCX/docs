The widget is the fastest channel to ship: one snippet on your site and visitors can chat with your AI agent immediately. The webchat channel is created automatically for every workspace, so there's nothing to "connect" — just paste the snippet.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grab your snippet">
    Go to **AI agents**, open the agent, select the **Channels** tab and click **Copy snippet** under **Embed snippet** in the **Web widget** card. Each AI agent has its own widget and snippet. It looks like this:

    ```html
    <script>
    (function(){
    window.Keloa=typeof window.Keloa==='function'?window.Keloa:(function(o){var f=function(){f.q.push(arguments)};f.q=(o&&o.q)||[];f.init=function(c){f.q.push(c)};return f})(window.Keloa);
    var s=document.createElement('script');
    s.src='https://YOUR_WIDGET_HOST/widget/v1/loader.js?agent=YOUR_AGENT_ID';
    if(document.readyState==='complete'){document.body.appendChild(s)}
    else{window.addEventListener('load',function(){document.body.appendChild(s)})}
    })();
    </script>
    ```

    Always copy the snippet from Keloa: it holds the exact loader URL. The `agent` value in that URL is your agent ID. The first line defines `Keloa(…)`, so your page can call it before the widget has loaded.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste before &lt;/body&gt;">
    Drop the snippet just before the closing `</body>` tag of your site. Every page that includes it will show the widget.

    Framework-specific notes:
    - **WordPress** — Appearance → Theme File Editor → `footer.php`, or any insert-header plugin.
    - **Webflow / Squarespace / Wix** — Settings → Custom code → Footer.
    - **Next.js** — `app/layout.tsx` inside `<body>`, or use `next/script` with `strategy="afterInteractive"`.
    - **Shopify (theme)** — `theme.liquid` before `</body>`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test">
    Open your site in an incognito window. A chat bubble appears in the bottom-right (or bottom-left, if you changed it). Click it, say hi, and watch the conversation arrive in your **Inbox**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Don't want to touch your site yet? The **Live preview** in the agent's **Web widget** card runs the widget on a sandboxed host page that mirrors your saved settings.
</Tip>

## Configuration

Each AI agent has its own widget. Everything below is set per agent: go to **AI agents**, open the agent and select the **Channels** tab. The settings live in the **Web widget** card. Changes save automatically (the card shows **Auto-saving**, then **Saved**) and reach your site on the next loader fetch (CDN cache 60 seconds).

### Appearance

- **Brand color** — bubble and header background. Pick a colour or paste a hex value.
- **Launcher position** — **Right** (default) or **Left**.
- **Header display name** — the name in the widget header. Falls back to the AI agent's name.
- **Welcome message** — the greeting shown when no intro bubbles are set. Leave it empty to use the built-in greeting.
- **Quick questions** — up to 6 chips that visitors can tap on a fresh chat instead of typing. Auto-seeded from your onboarding site scan and editable any time.
- **Business-hours message** — shown when the team is offline.

The widget avatar comes from the agent's **Profile** tab, with the brand logo as fallback.

### Intro bubbles

Up to 5 short "hello" bubbles shown when the widget opens. If the list is empty, the **Welcome message** is used instead.

### Privacy policy URL

Optional link in the small footer of the widget. Use it to point at your privacy policy.

### Allowed origins

Set per AI agent, under **AI agents → your agent → Channels → Web widget → Allowed origins**. Empty means the widget loads on any site that includes the snippet. Add hosts to restrict it, which helps when a third party copies your snippet onto an unrelated site.

- Enter a host without `https://`, a port or a path: `www.acme.com`. An exact host matches only itself, so `acme.com` doesn't cover `www.acme.com`.
- `*.acme.com` matches every subdomain of `acme.com`, but not `acme.com` itself.
- Only add a wildcard for a domain you control. Keloa refuses wildcards over public suffixes (from the [Public Suffix List](https://publicsuffix.org)) and over known shared-hosting platforms, such as `*.com`, `*.nl`, `*.co.uk`, `*.vercel.app`, `*.github.io` or `*.myshopify.com`. Keloa can't know every hosting platform, so a wildcard it accepts isn't proof that you control every site under it. For a site on a hosting platform, add its exact address (`acme.vercel.app`).
- A host with non-ASCII letters is saved in its punycode form, which is what browsers send: `*.bücher.de` becomes `*.xn--bcher-kva.de`.

With [identity verification](/developers/identity-verification) on, at least one allowed origin is required, and only https pages of these sites, on the standard https port (443), can show the widget. For development, `localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `*.test` and `*.localhost` also work over http and on any port.

### Working hours (Business+)

Working hours configured in **Settings → Workspace → Business hours** are visible to the AI via the system prompt and surfaced in the widget when the customer asks about availability. Outside hours, the AI sets expectations on response time rather than promising live help.

The hours field is a per-day window with a workspace timezone (`Europe/Amsterdam` by default). Enabling and editing this field requires the `working_hours` cap, available on Business and Scale plans.

## Loading behaviour

- The loader script is small, served `async`, and does nothing until the bubble is hovered or clicked.
- The full widget iframe is lazy-loaded the first time the visitor interacts.
- A first-party session token is stored in `localStorage` so returning visitors keep their conversation context. No cross-site cookies.
- Conversations of [signed-in users](/developers/identity-verification) are never stored in the browser; they load from Keloa after the user's token is verified.
- The public config endpoint (`/widget/v1/config`) is cached for 60 seconds at the edge.

## Advanced

### Identify signed-in users

If your site or app has accounts, your backend can sign a short-lived token that tells the widget who the signed-in user is. Keloa verifies it on every request, so nobody can chat as one of your users. Your team sees the user's verified details in the inbox, and their conversations follow them across devices.

```html
<script>
  window.Keloa=typeof window.Keloa==='function'?window.Keloa:(function(o){var f=function(){f.q.push(arguments)};f.q=(o&&o.q)||[];f.init=function(c){f.q.push(c)};return f})(window.Keloa);
  Keloa('identify', { getToken: () => fetchTokenFromYourBackend() });
</script>
```

Keloa only accepts identity in a token signed with your secret: an `identify` call with just an email or name is ignored. See [Identity verification](/developers/identity-verification) for setup, server code samples and security guidance.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Bubble doesn't appear | Right-click → Inspect → look for `loader.js` in the Network tab. Verify the `agent` value in the loader URL matches your AI agent. An unknown or paused agent returns `404`. |
| Widget blocked on your site | The console shows `[Keloa] Widget blocked: origin '…' is not in the allowed domains list.` Add the exact host under **Allowed origins**. With identity verification on, the page must also be served over https on the standard port (development hosts such as `localhost` excepted). |
| Signed-in users chat anonymously | Check the `reason` in the `401 identity_invalid` response. See [Identity verification troubleshooting](/developers/identity-verification#troubleshooting). |
| Bubble appears but won't open | A CSP header is blocking the iframe. Allow `app.keloa.ai` (or your widget CDN host) as a frame source. |
| Messages land but no AI reply | Check that the AI agent whose ID is in the loader URL is live, not paused. |
| Branding toggle is greyed out | You're not on Business or Scale, or your plan was just upgraded and the page hasn't reloaded yet. |
