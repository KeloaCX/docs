Every place where you receive customer messages is a **channel**. Keloa is live on web chat, email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger and Telegram. Every conversation lands in the same inbox, and your AI agent answers on all of them.

## Where to connect channels

Sidebar → **Integrations**. The **Channels** category holds **Email domain**, **Facebook Messenger**, **Instagram**, **WhatsApp** and **Telegram** (plus **Slack**, for teammates). The web widget is installed from **Settings → Widget**.

Connecting and disconnecting channels needs the **owner** or **admin** role. Other teammates see the channels but can't change them.

## Channel status

Each connected Page, number or bot has a card with its own status:

| Channel | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | **Live**, **Needs attention**. While Keloa sets up a number: **Requested**, **In progress** or **Declined**. See [WhatsApp](/channels/whatsapp#the-numbers-card). |
| **Messenger** and **Instagram** | **Connected**, **Reconnect needed**, **Disconnected**, **Pending**. See [Instagram](/channels/instagram#the-accounts-card). |
| **Telegram** | **Connected**, **Needs attention**, **Token revoked**. See [Telegram](/channels/telegram#the-bots-card). |

## Reply windows

Some messaging apps only let businesses reply for a limited time after the contact's last message. Keloa locks the reply box and pauses the AI agent when a window closes.

| Channel | Reply window |
| --- | --- |
| **Web widget**, **email** | None |
| **Telegram** | None. Reply any time, unless the contact blocked the bot. |
| **WhatsApp** | 24 hours after the contact's last message (72 hours after a click-to-WhatsApp ad). |
| **Messenger** and **Instagram** | 24 hours. With the optional **Extended reply window (24h–7d)**, teammates (not the AI agent) can reply for up to 7 days. |

## Channel limits per plan

| Plan | Channels | Email channels | Email domains | Integrations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 1 | — (locked) | — (locked) | 1 |
| Growth | 2 | 3 | 1 | 5 |
| Business | 5 | 6 | 3 | 15 |
| Scale | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

**Channels**, **email channels**, **email domains** and **integrations** are four separate pools: they don't share a cap. The **Channels** cap counts your web widget and email inboxes. An **email channel** is one email address you reply as; it also counts as a channel. An **email domain** is a verified sending domain. An **integration** is a connected third-party app (Shopify, custom HTTP, Slack, Google Drive) that feeds data or runs actions; see [Core concepts](/concepts#integration). Two email addresses on the same domain is two email channels but one verified domain.

WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and Telegram have their own limits and don't count against **Channels**:

| Plan | Messenger and Instagram | WhatsApp numbers | Telegram bots |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | — (locked) | — (locked) | — (locked) |
| Growth | Included | 1 | 1 |
| Business | Included | 3 | 3 |
| Scale | Included | Unlimited | Unlimited |

<Note>
Email is a **Growth+** feature. Starter is widget-only: `email_channels` and `email_domains` are locked on Starter. Upgrade to Growth to add email channels.
</Note>

## One AI agent per channel

Every connected Page, number and bot has one **AI agent** that answers it. Pick it under **Answered by** on the card in **Integrations**, or in the agent editor under **Channels** → **Social channels**. Use flows to override the default for specific senders or topics.

Whatever the channel, the AI agent reads the whole conversation, not only the latest message. See [Conversation memory](/agents/overview#conversation-memory).

## Real-time

Every channel feeds the same real-time **Inbox**. Teammates see new messages within a second.

## Privacy

All channel traffic is stored in the EU. When you disconnect a channel, inbound messages stop immediately. Historical conversations stay unless you delete them in **Settings → Data &amp; privacy**.
