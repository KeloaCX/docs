Tune how the widget looks on your site — colour, welcome message, intro bubbles, quick questions, position, and branding. The **Live preview** next to the settings shows what you saved.

## Open widget settings

Each AI agent has its own widget. Go to **AI agents**, open the agent and select the **Channels** tab. The settings live in the **Web widget** card. **Settings → Widget** only points there now.

## What you can customise

| Setting | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand color** | Bubble and header background. Pick a colour or paste a hex value. |
| **Launcher position** | **Right** (default) or **Left**. |
| **Header display name** | The name in the widget header. Falls back to the AI agent's name. |
| **Welcome message** | The greeting shown when no intro bubbles are set. Empty = the built-in greeting. |
| **Quick questions** | Up to 6 chips that visitors can tap on a fresh chat. Auto-seeded from your onboarding site scan. |
| **Business-hours message** | Shown when the team is offline. |
| **Allowed origins** | Empty = any site. Add hosts (`www.acme.com`) to restrict the widget to your sites. Only add a wildcard (`*.acme.com`) for a domain you control: wildcards over public suffixes and known shared-hosting platforms (`*.co.uk`, `*.vercel.app`) are refused. See [Allowed origins](/channels/web-widget#allowed-origins). |
| **Intro bubbles** | Up to 5 short "hello" bubbles shown when the widget opens. |
| **Privacy policy URL (optional)** | Link in the small footer of the widget. |
| **Branding** | A switch that hides the "Powered by Keloa" badge in the footer. **Business+ only.** |

The widget avatar is the AI agent's avatar, set on the agent's **Profile** tab. The brand logo is the fallback.

## Plan-gated features

Two widget capabilities are locked behind plan caps:

- **Branding** (hide the Keloa badge) — requires the `widget_unbranded` cap. Available on Business and Scale.
- **Working hours integration** — requires the `working_hours` cap. Available on Business and Scale. Configure in **Settings → Workspace → Business hours**.

See [Billing & plans](/billing/plans) for the full cap matrix.

## Preview

The **Live preview** next to the settings runs the widget on a sandboxed host page (`/widget/host-demo`) with your saved settings. It updates when you save — useful for clicking through a real conversation without touching your site. **Send test message** next to the embed snippet checks that the agent answers.

## Languages

The widget greeting is translated automatically into English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish. The visitor's locale is detected from `navigator.language`, with the brand language as fallback. The **Welcome message** you type in the agent's **Web widget** card is used as the operator-customised version for your brand's primary locale.

## Accessibility

- **WCAG 2.2 AA** contrast on default theme colours.
- The launcher is focusable via keyboard (`Tab`) and the chat panel is operable with arrow keys.
- Screen reader announcements fire on every state change.

## Mobile behaviour

- The widget opens full-screen on mobile breakpoints.
- The launcher offset honours iOS safe areas.

## Identifying logged-in users

If your site authenticates visitors, your backend can sign a token that tells the widget who the signed-in user is. See [Identity verification](/developers/identity-verification).

## Save

Changes save automatically: the card shows **Auto-saving**, then **Saved**. The next loader fetch on your site picks up the change (CDN cache 60 seconds).
