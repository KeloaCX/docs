The first time you sign in, Keloa runs a short wizard that turns a blank workspace into something usable. This page shows every step in detail so you know what's happening and where to revisit each piece afterwards.

## Step 1 · Website scan

Paste a URL and Keloa inspects the homepage to extract:

| What we detect | Where it lands |
| --- | --- |
| Brand name and logo colour | **Settings → Workspace** and the widget theme |
| Industry and short description | **Settings → Workspace → Context** (used in agent prompts) |
| Default language | **Settings → Workspace** |
| Support email | **Settings → Workspace → Contact** |
| Business hours | **Settings → Workspace → Contact** |
| Starter system prompt | Your first AI agent |

<Note>
Nothing is saved until you click **Next**. You can also **Skip** the scan entirely and fill it in by hand.
</Note>

## Step 2 · Your first AI agent

Every workspace needs at least one agent. Onboarding creates one with:

- **Name** — what customers see ("Nova", "Kay", whatever fits)
- **Tone** — Neutral / Friendly / Formal — maps to the system prompt
- **Greeting** — the first message on the widget (e.g. *"Hi! How can I help?"*)
- **System prompt** — drafted from the scan; you can edit it now or later

The agent starts as **live** — it will answer immediately on any channel you connect.

See [Create an agent](/agents/create-agent) to change these later.

## Step 3 · Try it

When you click **Finish setup**, Keloa starts reading your website and opens a **Try it** chat. Ask your agent anything a real customer might ask; citations show where the answer came from. The page count shows how much of your site is indexed so far. Test chats don't appear in your inbox.

Click **Go to dashboard** when you're done, or **Skip — go to dashboard** to leave right away.

## After onboarding · Connect your channels

Onboarding doesn't connect a channel. Add them from the dashboard whenever you're ready:

  <Card title="Web widget" icon="comment" href="/channels/web-widget">
    Fastest to set up: one script tag on your site, from **Settings → Widget**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope" href="/channels/email">
    Forward support@yours to Keloa; replies go out from your verified domain. Growth and above.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WhatsApp" icon="whatsapp" href="/channels/whatsapp">
    Request your number under **Integrations** and Keloa sets it up with you. Growth and above.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Instagram and Messenger" icon="facebook-messenger" href="/channels/messenger">
    Connect your Facebook Page and its Instagram account with your Facebook login. Growth and above. See also [Instagram](/channels/instagram).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Telegram" icon="telegram" href="/channels/telegram">
    Paste your Telegram bot's token under **Integrations**. Growth and above.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Slack" icon="slack" href="/channels/slack">
    Install the Keloa app in your Slack workspace under **Integrations**. Every plan, using one integration slot.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
Shopify is an **integration** (orders, products, knowledge sync), not a chat channel. Connect it from **Integrations** any time — see [Shopify knowledge source](/knowledge/shopify).
</Tip>

## Completing onboarding

When you click **Finish setup**, Keloa:

1. Marks your workspace as onboarded so you won't land here again.
2. Creates a live AI agent.
3. Queues a knowledge ingest job for your website (you'll see pages appearing in **Knowledge** within a minute or two).

After the **Try it** chat you land on the **Dashboard**.

## Coming back to a step

| I want to… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Change workspace name, colour, or hours | **Settings → Workspace** |
| Rerun or edit the website scan | **Knowledge → Add source → Website crawl** |
| Edit the first AI agent | **AI agents → ⋯ → Edit** |
| Add more channels | **Integrations** |
| Restyle the widget | **AI agents → your agent → Channels → Web widget** |

<Tip>
If you ever want to redo onboarding: clear it with your admin, or just open **Knowledge** and start a new crawl — that covers 90% of what onboarding did.
</Tip>
