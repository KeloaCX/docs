## Handoff node

A **Handoff** node hands the conversation to your team and tells the AI to step back. It ends the flow: nothing can come after it.

### Hand off to

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Specific member** | The conversation goes to this member, even if someone else has it. If they can no longer take conversations, the least busy teammate gets it. |
| **A team** | The team's own assignment method decides who gets it (round-robin, least busy, or manual). Someone on the team who already has the conversation keeps it. A team that assigns manually keeps it in the team queue and alerts the whole team. |
| **Least busy** | The teammate with the fewest open conversations gets it. |
| **Round-robin across everyone** | All teammates take turns: whoever was handed a conversation longest ago is next. |

With **Least busy** and **Round-robin across everyone**, a conversation someone already owns stays with them, and one already routed to a team (for example by an [assignment rule](/settings/assignment-rules)) stays with that team.

Create teams in **Settings → Teams**. If the team a Handoff node points to is deleted, the node hands off as **Least busy** until you pick another team, and you can't publish until you do.

### Reason

Add a short **Reason / note** (*"VIP customer"*, *"Complex billing issue"*). It's added to the conversation as an internal note and sent with the hand-off notification, so the assignee knows why it landed with them.

### What handoff actually does

- Assigns the conversation to the chosen member or team, and notifies them.
- Stops the AI and the flow from replying until the conversation is solved or closed.
- If the flow sent no Message before the Handoff, the AI agent writes a short acknowledgement for the contact. To say something specific instead, put a **Message** node right before the Handoff.

### When the AI takes over again

After a hand-off, your team answers. Once the conversation is solved or closed, a brand-new question from the contact is the AI's again.

## Resolve node

A **Resolve** node marks the conversation as solved. It ends the flow: nothing can come after it.

### Configure

- **Resolution** — Resolved / Spam / No action needed / Duplicate. Recorded on the conversation.
- **Satisfaction survey** — switch on **Always send a satisfaction survey** to survey every conversation this step solves.

### When it solves

- If a **Message** comes before the Resolve, the conversation is solved once that reply is actually sent. A reply that stays a draft or can't be sent keeps the conversation open for your team.
- If that Message came out empty (the AI couldn't write it), the Resolve hands the conversation to your team instead, with an internal note saying why.
- A conversation that is already solved or closed is left as it is.

### CSAT

With **Always send a satisfaction survey** on, the contact gets a survey with five faces when this step solves the conversation, even if they had one recently or the AI agent solved it. Surveys still need to be on for the channel in **Settings → Customer satisfaction**, and the delay set there applies.

With it off, your usual survey rules in **Settings → Customer satisfaction** decide, as when a teammate solves a conversation.

The survey goes out on the conversation's own channel: a rating card in website chat, an email, a message with a link on Messenger and Instagram, or a message with five faces on Telegram and in Slack direct messages. WhatsApp conversations and Slack channel threads aren't surveyed. See [CSAT](/analytics/csat).

In a [follow-up flow](/flows/follow-up-flows), a Resolve node solves the conversation right away and never sends a satisfaction survey.

## Combining handoff and resolve

A flow path ends in one or the other. One flow can use both on different branches:

```
Trigger (Every customer message)
  → Intent (thanks, billing, other)
      ├── thanks  → Message (Compose with AI: "Say you're glad we could help.")
      │            → Resolve (resolved)
      ├── billing → Handoff (A team: Billing, reason "Billing question")
      └── other   → (not connected: the AI answers)
```

A contact who just says thanks gets a short reply and the conversation is solved once it's sent; billing questions go to the billing team; everything else is answered by the AI.

## Important

- A **Handoff** and a **Resolve** end the flow. If you want a reassurance message, put the **Message** before the Handoff.
- **Resolve** is the actual solve. If you don't want to solve the conversation, end the path without a Resolve node.
