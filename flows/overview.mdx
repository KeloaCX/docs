title: "Flows overview"

A **flow** is a visual automation you build by dragging nodes onto a canvas and connecting them. Flows add fixed, predictable logic on top of your AI agent: route a conversation to the right person, send a set reply, or follow up when a contact doesn't answer your email.

## Two kinds of flow

When you click **New flow**, you pick what the flow should do. You can't change this later.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Reply flow" icon="comments">
    Answers incoming messages with your AI agent. It runs when a contact writes, before the AI answers, and can route, reply, hand off or solve.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follow-up flow" icon="envelope" href="/flows/follow-up-flows">
    Follows up by email after your team replies, and stops when the contact answers. For example: reminders after you send a price.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A reply flow belongs to one AI agent and only runs in conversations that agent answers. An agent runs one reply flow at a time. A follow-up flow belongs to the whole workspace and doesn't need an agent.

## When to use a flow

- **Routing** — hand mail from a key account straight to its account manager.
- **Topic triage** — let the AI sort the first message into *sales*, *billing* or *support*, and hand sales and billing to the right team.
- **Auto-replies** — send a set greeting on the first message of a conversation on one channel.
- **After-hours** — send a short note when a conversation starts outside business hours.
- **Follow-ups** — remind a contact about the proposal you sent, and stop as soon as they answer. See [Follow-up flows](/flows/follow-up-flows).

You don't need a flow for the AI to answer — that happens automatically once an agent is attached to a channel. When a reply flow ends without sending anything, the AI answers as usual.

## Node types

Reply flows use these nodes:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trigger" icon="bolt">
    The start. Fires on a new conversation, every customer message, a reopened conversation or a new contact.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Condition" icon="code-branch">
    Branch on channel, mailbox, subject, message text, tag, team, contact details, language, business hours or a custom field.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Intent" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    The AI sorts the latest customer message into one of the intents you list; the flow follows that output.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Message" icon="comment">
    Reply with your own text, or let the AI write it from your instructions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Action" icon="wrench">
    Run one of your workspace's tools, such as a Shopify order lookup.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Handoff" icon="user-plus">
    Hand the conversation to a member, a team, the least busy teammate or the next one in the rotation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Resolve" icon="circle-check">
    Mark the conversation as solved, optionally with a satisfaction survey.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Follow-up flows use **Trigger**, **Condition** and **Resolve** too, plus three nodes of their own: **Wait for a reply**, **Send follow-up** and **Update ticket**. See [Follow-up flows](/flows/follow-up-flows).

## Plan availability

Flows are available from the **Growth** plan upwards. Reply flows and follow-up flows count toward the same cap:

| Plan | Flows |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | — |
| Growth | 2 |
| Business | 6 |
| Scale | Unlimited |

Only workspace admins can create and change flows. Other teammates see them read-only.

## Typical flow shapes

**Key-account routing:**
```
Trigger (New conversation, channel: Email)
  → Condition (Sender domain equals "acme.com")
      ├── Acme      → Handoff (Specific member: the account manager)
      └── Otherwise → (not connected: the AI answers)
```

**After-hours note:**
```
Trigger (New conversation)
  → Condition (Business hours equals "closed")
      ├── Closed    → Message ("Thanks! Our team is back at 9:00 and will reply then.")
      └── Otherwise → (not connected: the AI answers)
```

**Topic triage** (the starter every new reply flow begins with):
```
Trigger (New conversation)
  → Intent (support, sales, billing, other)
      ├── sales   → Handoff (reason: sales inquiry)
      ├── billing → Handoff (reason: billing question)
      └── support, other → (not connected: the AI answers)
```

**Proposal follow-up** (the starter every new follow-up flow begins with):
```
Trigger (When your team replies)
  → Condition (Your team's reply contains "€")
  → Wait for a reply (3 working days)
      └── No reply → Send follow-up → Wait for a reply → Send follow-up
                   → Wait for a reply → Update ticket (tag, note, reopen for the owner)
```
