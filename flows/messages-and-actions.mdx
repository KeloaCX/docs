## Message nodes

A **Message** node replies to the contact, on the same channel the conversation is on. It's sent right away, as your AI agent's reply to the message that started the flow.

### Configure

- **Message template** — the text, with variable substitution (e.g. `{{contact.first_name}}`). Click a variable under the field to insert it.
- **Compose with AI** — switch it on to let the AI write the message instead. Fill in **Instructions for the AI** (*"Acknowledge the customer warmly, tell them a specialist will reply shortly. Keep it short."*). The AI writes one to three short sentences in the contact's language, based on your instructions and the conversation so far. Useful for hand-off acknowledgements where a fixed template would feel robotic.

If the AI can't write the message, nothing is sent. A **Resolve** node after it then hands the conversation to your team instead of solving it, so the contact is never left without an answer.

### One reply per message

A reply flow sends at most one message per customer message. If a path passes through more than one Message node, the last one is sent.

### Variables

A flow message can use these contact variables. A variable without a value renders empty.

| Variable | Renders |
| --- | --- |
| `{{contact.first_name}}` | Contact's first name |
| `{{contact.name}}` | Full name |
| `{{contact.company}}` | Contact's company name |
| `{{contact.email}}` | Contact's email address. On website conversations, see below. |
| `{{contact.phone}}` | Contact's phone number. On website conversations, see below. |

Flows don't fill `{{contact.last_name}}` or the other [macro](/inbox/macros) variables, such as `{{account.name}}`. In a flow message they render empty.

#### Contact details on website conversations

On the web widget, Keloa can't tell who is typing unless your site signs them in with [identity verification](/developers/identity-verification). So a flow message on a website conversation only shows contact details the person chatting is known to own:

- `{{contact.email}}` shows the address when the visitor typed it in that conversation (in a message or the contact form) or in the contact form of one of their own earlier conversations your team merged into it, or when it is the verified email your site signed for the signed-in user of that conversation.
- `{{contact.phone}}` shows the number when the visitor typed it in that conversation, or when your site signed it for the signed-in user of that conversation.

Otherwise the placeholder renders empty, so on an anonymous conversation it stays empty unless the visitor typed the detail there.

Names follow the same rule. On an anonymous website conversation, `{{contact.name}}`, `{{contact.first_name}}` and `{{contact.company}}` only show a name the visitor gave in that conversation, in the chat or in the contact form, or in the contact form of one of their own earlier conversations your team merged into it; otherwise they render empty, because a name your team, an email or a merge put on the contact may be someone else's. Contact and company custom fields count as unknown there too. On a signed-in user's conversation, they show the name and company your site signed for that user.

Signed-in users' conversations follow the rules above for email and phone. Other channels, such as email and WhatsApp, render every placeholder as usual.

The same rules apply wherever a flow reads the contact on a website conversation: [Condition nodes](/flows/conditions#contact-fields-on-website-conversations), and `{{contact.*}}` values you pass to an action. A detail the visitor isn't shown to own counts as empty there too.

### Channel quirks

- The message goes out like your AI agent's other replies, so the agent's sending settings apply. On **email**, a reply the agent would only draft stays a draft for your team to send.
- **WhatsApp**, **Messenger** and **Instagram** only deliver within the reply window (24 hours after the contact's last message; 72 hours after a click-to-WhatsApp ad). A Message node on a conversation whose window has closed isn't delivered.
- **Telegram** and **Slack** have no reply window.
- **Email** messages go as a reply in the existing thread.
- **Webchat** messages appear with the agent's avatar.

## Action nodes

An **Action** node runs one tool, then the flow goes on to the next node. It sends nothing to the contact by itself.

### Configure

- **Tool** — pick one of the tools your workspace has.
- **Parameters** — values the tool needs, if any. A value can be a contact variable such as `{{contact.email}}`.

### Available tools

The **Tool** list depends on what your workspace has connected:

| Tool | Available when |
| --- | --- |
| **Get Shopify order**, **Search Shopify product** | A Shopify store is linked to one of your AI agents |
| **Get WooCommerce order**, **Search WooCommerce products** | A WooCommerce store is linked to one of your AI agents |
| **Look up TRONVoice invoices**, **quotes**, **subscriptions** | TRONVoice is connected |
| **Search the web** | Your plan includes web search |
| **Custom HTTP call** | Always |
| **Hand off to a human** | Always |

The tool runs as the flow's AI agent. A store tool only runs when that agent is linked to the store with the tool switched on (**AI agents → Skills → Store**). If a tool can't run, the flow carries on to the next node.

<Tip>
To hand a conversation to your team, use a [Handoff node](/flows/handoff-and-resolve) rather than the **Hand off to a human** tool: the Handoff node assigns the conversation and lets you pick who gets it.
</Tip>

### Chaining

Each Action node has one output, so you can put several in a row. In the tester, tools aren't called: the panel shows *Would run "…" (not called in tests)*, and the steps after it see an empty result.

## Example: after-hours note

```
Trigger (New conversation, channel: any)
  → Condition (Business hours equals "closed")
      ├── Closed    → Message ("Thanks! Our team replies when we reopen at 9:00.")
      └── Otherwise → (not connected: the AI answers)
```

Outside business hours the contact gets a friendly acknowledgement as the reply to their first message. Inside business hours the flow ends and the AI answers as usual. Set your hours in [Workspace settings](/settings/workspace); with no hours set, the workspace counts as always open.

## Example: AI acknowledgement before a hand-off

```
Trigger (New conversation)
  → Intent (billing, other)
      ├── billing → Message (Compose with AI: "Tell them our billing team will reply today.")
      │            → Handoff (A team: Billing, reason "Billing question")
      └── other   → (not connected: the AI answers)
```

The contact gets a short, natural reply in their own language, and the conversation lands with the billing team.
