We'll build a classic reply flow: **"When someone from a key account emails us, hand the conversation to their account manager."**

<Note>
This page builds a **reply flow**. To send reminders after your team replies, see [Follow-up flows](/flows/follow-up-flows).
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the flow">
    Sidebar → **Flows** → **New flow**.

    Under **What should this flow do?** pick **Reply flow**, give it a name (*Acme routing*) and click **Create flow**.

    A new reply flow starts with a starter flow: **New conversation** → **Classify intent** → hand-offs for sales and billing. Select the nodes you don't need and remove them, or build on them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the AI agent">
    At the top of the canvas, pick the AI agent this flow belongs to. The flow only runs in conversations that agent answers; next to the picker you see what it answers (its mailboxes and web chat).

    Each agent runs one reply flow at a time. Publishing this flow later moves the agent's other published reply flow back to draft.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the Trigger">
    Click the **Trigger** node to configure it:
    - **Channel** — Email
    - **Event** — New conversation (first message)

    (Leave **Channel** on **Any channel** to run on every channel the agent answers.)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a Condition">
    Drag **Condition** from the palette on the left onto the canvas (or click it) and connect Trigger → Condition.

    Click it to configure **Branch 1**:
    - **Field** — Sender domain
    - **Operator** — equals
    - **Value** — `acme.com`

    Rename the branch to *Acme* if you like. A Condition node has one output per branch, plus **Otherwise** for everything that matches no branch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a Handoff">
    Drag **Handoff** onto the canvas. Connect the *Acme* output to it.

    Configure:
    - **Hand off to** — Specific member
    - **Assign to** — the account manager
    - **Reason / note** — *"Acme key account"* (added to the conversation as an internal note)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let the AI handle the rest">
    Leave **Otherwise** unconnected. When the condition doesn't match, the flow simply ends — and the AI agent answers as usual.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and test">
    Click **Save** (or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>). The flow is saved as a **draft** — it doesn't run yet.

    Click **Test**. On the **Scenario** tab set **Channel** to Email and **Email** to an `@acme.com` address, then send a message on the **Chat** tab. The path the message takes lights up on the canvas.

    See [Publish and test](/flows/publish-and-test) for the full testing workflow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish">
    When the test looks right, click **Publish**. The flow goes live immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Canvas basics

- **Drag** from a node's output to another node to connect them.
- **Click** a node to open its settings panel on the right; <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.
- To remove a node, click **Delete** in its settings panel. Removing a node also removes its connections.
- **Drag** the empty canvas to pan; scroll or use the zoom buttons in the bottom-right corner to zoom. **Fit view** shows the whole flow.
- <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> saves, <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes and <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> redoes.

## Auto-layout

Messy canvas? Click **Auto-layout** to let Keloa tidy the nodes based on their connections.

## Compose with AI

In a reply flow, click **Compose with AI** and describe the flow in plain English, for example *"if shopping → Tayfun, technical questions → the Technical team, otherwise let the AI handle it."* Keloa builds the nodes for you and replaces the canvas (undo with <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>). Review the result, then save.

## Editing a published flow

Saving changes to a published flow puts them live straight away. To take a flow offline, click **Unpublish**: it goes back to draft and stops running, and you can publish it again any time.

## Common flow patterns

- **Welcome message** — Trigger (New conversation) → Message ("Hi, welcome to Acme! How can we help?")
- **After-hours** — Trigger (New conversation) → Condition (Business hours equals closed) → Message ("We're back at 9:00")
- **Route by topic** — Trigger (New conversation) → Intent (sales, billing, support) → Handoff for sales and billing
- **Route by mailbox** — Trigger (New conversation, Email) → Condition (Sent to contains `sales@`) → Handoff (A team: Sales)
